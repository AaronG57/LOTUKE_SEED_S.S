Seed School Model

Established as part of the Ministry of Education's initiative to ensure equity and access to secondary education in underserved rural sub-counties.

Academic Structure

Operates lower secondary (Ordinary Level / UCE) curriculum supervised directly under Uganda National Examinations Board (UNEB) standards.

MoES Compliance

Fully recognized and integrated into the national Education Management Information System (EMIS Code: 188003) for rural development planning.
